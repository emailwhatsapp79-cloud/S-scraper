Sides are never hardcoded. The favourite is whichever full-time home or away leg is shorter; every check is written against fav and dog, so an away-favourite is screened the same way, mirrored.

01 · FT 1X2

Favourite in a band, dog long

Favourite between 1.50 and 2.50. The other side at least 3.70, no ceiling. A level game dies here.

02 · Double chance

Favourite-or-draw tight, dog-or-draw long

1X (or X2 if away is favourite) between 1.17 and 1.30. The other double-chance at least 1.89. This is the natural back if a game clears.

03 · Half time

Split, not a coin-flip

Favourite’s half-time 1X2 between 1.10 and 3.00, and the other half-time leg at least 1.70× that price. Check six is what throws out the level games that look fine at full time.

04 · Upcoming only

If it has kicked off, it cannot be backed

The site reads the rest of today’s vFootball card from SportyBet over GET. Live games are recorded, then dropped from Matches. Nothing is placed on an account.
